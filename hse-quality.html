Safety and quality on every project

Nobody gets hurt. Work is planned, controlled, tested and recorded.

Our approach

Health, safety, environmental protection and quality are essential to responsible engineering delivery.

We plan work to identify hazards, apply appropriate controls, verify the quality of completed activities and maintain records required by the project. Our work is carried out in line with applicable regulations, client procedures and approved project documentation.

  • HSE commitment and management responsibilities
  • Risk assessment and job safety analysis, where applicable
  • Permit-to-work and isolation / lockout-tagout requirements, where applicable to site rules
  • Toolbox talks, competency checks and site inductions
  • PPE, emergency preparedness and incident reporting
  • Environmental controls, waste handling and spill prevention appropriate to the work
  • Inspection and test plans, inspection records, test packs and punch-list management
  • Document control, non-conformance reporting and corrective action
  • Compliance with applicable laws, client requirements, approved codes and standards
