About Vertex Engineering Services Limited

Hands-on field experience, disciplined project management and a firm commitment to safety.

Company overview

Vertex Engineering Services Limited is an engineering and technical services company specialising in the installation, pre-commissioning and commissioning of electrical and mechanical systems for the oil and gas, power and industrial sectors.

We support gas turbines, gas generators and associated balance-of-plant systems, as well as mechanical piping and electrical works, from site mobilisation through to successful start-up and handover.

Our teams combine hands-on field experience with disciplined project management, adherence to applicable codes and standards, and a firm commitment to health, safety and environmental protection. Whether supporting an upstream or downstream oil and gas facility, a new power plant or a plant expansion, we aim to deliver work safely, to the agreed schedule and scope, and with long-term reliability in mind.

Our mission

To deliver safe, high-quality installation, pre-commissioning and commissioning services that help clients in the oil and gas, power and industrial sectors bring their facilities online efficiently and reliably. We achieve this through skilled professionals, sound engineering practice and an uncompromising focus on safety, quality and integrity.

Our vision

To be a leading and trusted engineering services company in the oil and gas, power and industrial sectors, recognised for technical excellence, dependable project delivery and long-term partnerships with our clients.

Our core values

Safety First

Nobody gets hurt. We plan and execute every task with safety as the priority, especially in high-risk operating environments.

Quality

We work to recognised requirements, follow approved procedures and test thoroughly before handover.

Integrity

We aim to be honest, transparent and accountable in all our dealings.

Competence

We support training and competence development so personnel are prepared for their assigned responsibilities.

Reliability

We work to meet agreed commitments on time and within scope.

Client Focus

We listen, communicate clearly and tailor solutions to each client's requirements.

How we work

  1. Understand the scope, site conditions, interfaces and acceptance criteria.
  2. Plan people, materials, equipment, work methods, permits and risk controls.
  3. Execute work using approved documentation and competent personnel.
  4. Inspect, test, record results and resolve punch-list items.
  5. Compile handover documents and support start-up or close-out as agreed.
