Automation & Control Systems

  • Instrumentation
  • PLC
  • SCADA
  • Control Systems

Training in industrial automation, instrumentation, control systems, and troubleshooting to support efficient and reliable plant operations.

Key training areas

  • Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC) and SCADA fundamentals
  • Instrumentation, sensors, and control valves
  • Process control and industrial automation
  • Instrument calibration, fault-finding, and troubleshooting
  • Control panel operations and basic system diagnostics
  • Instrument loop checking and functional testing

Mechanical Piping & Fabrication

  • Pipefitting
  • Fabrication
  • Installation
  • Testing

Practical training in piping systems, fabrication techniques, installation procedures, and inspection practices used in industrial and oil and gas projects.

Key training areas

  • Pipefitting, isometric drawing interpretation, and spool fabrication
  • Pipe cutting, beveling, alignment, and fit-up
  • Welding fundamentals and fabrication quality control
  • Piping installation and support systems
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing and leak testing
  • Piping flushing, reinstatement, and pre-commissioning
  • Introduction to applicable piping codes and standards

Electrical Installation & Maintenance

  • Electrical Systems
  • Cable Works
  • Testing
  • Commissioning

Training focused on industrial electrical systems, installation techniques, testing procedures, and maintenance practices for safe and reliable operations.

Key training areas

  • Industrial electrical installation and wiring
  • Cable laying, glanding, termination, and identification
  • Electrical motors, starters, and motor control systems
  • Electrical testing, insulation resistance, and continuity checks
  • Electrical drawings and schematic interpretation
  • Electrical fault diagnosis and preventive maintenance
  • Electrical pre-commissioning, commissioning, and loop checks
  • Electrical safety, isolation, and lockout/tagout principles
